Keeping storage small

  • Photos are compressed to ~640px WebP at low quality (~15–40 KB). 1 GB fits roughly 30,000+ photos.
  • Movie posters are stored as links to IMDb images, not files — a watched entry is well under 1 KB.
  • Deleting a memory here also deletes its file from storage.
  • Reactions are tiny rows; even thousands take a few hundred KB.
