About me

Hey, I’m Shovo — I keep tech running and build what’s next.

I’m an IT & software person who likes both sides of the stack: the cables, networks and devices that keep a company moving, and the web apps that make the work easier.

By day I’m an IT Systems Administrator at Ketek Group Inc. in Edmonton — running servers, VMware and Active Directory, keeping the network secure, and helping people get unstuck. I started there in IT support and was promoted in 2026. Before that I built full-stack apps with Django, React and Next.js for WareMatch and Connection Hub, and ran a Subway store along the way, which taught me more about people and pressure than any textbook.

I studied Computing Science (Software Specialization) at the University of Alberta and hold two AWS certifications. Outside work I’m usually tinkering with a side project, writing on the blog below, or exploring Alberta with a camera.
